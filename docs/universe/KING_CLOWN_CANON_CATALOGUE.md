# King Clown Canon — expanded character catalogue

This add-on adds **20 profiles** from `king-clown-canon.zip`, in addition to the original 19 Kristal MEDIA profiles.

The original canon repository is the authoritative source. `CANON/` governs meaning; `CORPUS/` records source-specific stories that do not automatically override it.

## What is established vs imagined

- `extensions.canon.evidenceSummary` is a paraphrase of specific evidence from a source passage, with its exact archive-relative `sourceRefs`.
- Polarities, numerical scores, fears, motivations, growth arcs and any unspecified costume or expression are **creative proposals**, not facts fixed by the canon.
- Deities here are portrayals **within the supplied fictional Mythos corpus**, not claims about religious beliefs or living cultures.
- Réjean, Colin and the public King Klown persona belong to one identity continuity with different narrative functions.
- Aïda Éloïse is already present in the earlier pack, so she is not duplicated.
- π, AIME, kOA, KinCity and Reignbow are concepts, systems, places or organizations, not invented individual characters.
- Kristal Authoring Kit and Arketype are authoring/structuring toolkits; they do not add canonical persons.

## Safe use and rights

The supplied canon repository does **not** yet designate a public reuse licence. This Karacter add-on includes new short metadata summaries, not the full narrative corpus. Publication rights for source material and characters must be clarified by the project owner.

## Installation / import

On a fresh install, the added Canon profiles appear alongside the 19 Kristal MEDIA characters. For an existing install, press **Import King Klown Canon** in the sidebar. The operation is additive, rerunnable, and preserves your edited characters and portraits.

Profile source tags appear on the Create page.
