## Philosophy

Caution and boldness are not moral opposites. They are **contextual creative contrasts**: caution can protect or delay; boldness can open opportunities or create risks. Karacter's paired sliders help make those choices visible without labeling one pole a virtue and the other a flaw. They are not clinical scores.

**Language switch:** FR / EN changes the interface, not your own written character records.
