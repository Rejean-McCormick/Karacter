# Narrative design

The **Inner conflict and growth** section asks six questions: what does the character want, what do they need, which belief holds them back, what internal contradiction drives them, which turning point tests them, and how might they change?

All six fields are optional. They are intended for storytelling, **not diagnosis**. A character’s growth does not require changing polarity scores.

## King Klown example

King Klown wants to protect collective freedom and balance. He believes he must anticipate risks and secretly guide others. His contradiction is that controlling choices may undermine the freedom he wants to preserve. A possible turning point is being confronted by someone he has manipulated. His arc may involve learning to trust other people’s decisions.

Your own character’s story fields are saved in TXT character sheets and complete JSON/ZIP backups.
