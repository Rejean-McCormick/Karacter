# Archetypes and situations

## Twelve fictional archetypes

The **Archetypes** tab offers 12 character templates, such as the Dreamer, Protector, Observer, Trickster and Mediator.

- **Create** makes a new character based on a template.
- **Apply** replaces the active character's archetype-related settings, while keeping its name, collection and portraits.

These archetypes are creative starting points, not permanent psychological labels.

## Eight everyday situations

The **Situations** tab explores scenarios such as losing a game, making a mistake in front of others, disagreement, or unexpected changes. Karacter suggests what a character might **feel**, **think**, **do** and **need**. You can edit the answers and save them with the character or export one scenario as a TXT file. **Reset to initial ideas** removes the custom response for that scenario.

## A discussion tool

Different reactions may be plausible in the same situation. The point is to compare possibilities, understand emotions and consider context without labelling personalities as “good” or “bad”. These examples do not predict real behavior or diagnose mental health conditions.
