# Personality polarities

## Two linked poles

Each axis describes **two tendencies**, not a strength and a flaw. Each pole has a slider from **0 to 6**, and both values always add up to six:

| Caution | Boldness | Possible interpretation |
|---:|---:|---|
| 6 | 0 | Very strong caution emphasis |
| 5 | 1 | Clear caution emphasis |
| 4 | 2 | Slight caution emphasis |
| 3 | 3 | Both tendencies are equally present |
| 2 | 4 | Slight boldness emphasis |
| 1 | 5 | Clear boldness emphasis |
| 0 | 6 | Very strong boldness emphasis |

A score of **0 is not a failure** and **6 is not a mark of excellence**. These scores are storytelling controls, not psychometric measurements.

## Context matters

Caution may notice danger, or miss a timely opportunity. Boldness may invite discovery, or introduce avoidable risk. The same tendency can help or hinder depending on the situation. These are fictional contrasts; caution and courage are not necessarily mutually exclusive in real life.

## Editing an axis

1. Open **Studio** and locate **Personality polarities**.
2. Move either slider; the paired value changes automatically.
3. Use **Edit** to rename either pole and adjust its visual cues, benefits and trade-offs.
4. Use **Add a polarity (2 poles)** to introduce a custom axis, such as Independence ↔ Collaboration.

## Image prompts

Changing a slider updates the generated image prompt, including the contextual scores and pose/expression guidance. If you have manually edited the prompt, click **Regenerate** to rebuild it. The visual theme never changes the personality scores.
